FAQ
Who is Barsion?+

Barsion OÜ is an Estonian IT company covering the full technology lifecycle: from advisory and infrastructure management to development, cloud and security.

What services do you offer?+

Managed IT with 24/7 monitoring, technology consulting, security & compliance, web and mobile development, cloud services, business continuity and custom software.

Who do you work with?+

Our clients work in finance, healthcare, commerce, manufacturing, logistics and tourism, in Estonia and across Europe.

How does a project start?+

Write to us through the form and we'll set up a free intro call. We then agree on scope, pricing and timeline, and get to work.

How do you price your work?+

Depending on the project: fixed price, time & materials, or a monthly subscription. You always know exactly what you're paying for before we start.

How do you protect our data?+

We work to ISO/IEC 27001 principles and in full compliance with the GDPR. As an EU company, your data stays in Europe, under European rules.

Which technologies do you work with?+

Node.js, Python, Java and .NET; React, Vue and Flutter; AWS, Azure and Google Cloud; PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis. The choice always follows your needs.

Why choose Barsion?+

One partner for all of your IT, transparent pricing and the experience of Estonia's digital state, without big-firm bureaucracy.
