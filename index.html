FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is Barsiko and what does the company do?+

Barsiko is an Estonian IT consulting firm offering end-to-end services — from strategic IT advisory and infrastructure management to web & mobile development, cloud solutions and cybersecurity.

What specific services does Barsiko provide?+

We offer managed services with 24/7 monitoring, IT consulting & advisory, cybersecurity, web and mobile development, cloud services, risk assessment & management, and custom software development.

Which industries do you serve?+

We work across multiple sectors: finance, healthcare & pharmaceuticals, retail & e-commerce, manufacturing, transportation & logistics, as well as hospitality & tourism.

How do I start working with Barsiko?+

Submit a request through the form on this website and schedule a free consultation to discuss your needs. Once the scope is agreed, we move to project approval and kickoff.

How is pricing determined?+

Pricing is customised based on project scope, the chosen engagement model (fixed-price, time & materials, or subscription), complexity and the technologies required.

What security and privacy standards do you adhere to?+

We follow international standards such as ISO/IEC 27001 and the GDPR, complemented by our own stringent confidentiality policies. As an EU-based company, European data protection is at the core of how we work.

Which technologies and platforms do you specialise in?+

Backend: Java, .NET, Node.js, Python, PHP. Frontend: React, Angular, Vue.js. Mobile: React Native, Flutter. Cloud: AWS, Azure, GCP. Databases: PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Redis.

What sets Barsiko apart from other IT firms?+

A holistic approach, deep technical expertise, full transparency and flexible, client-focused engagement models — combined with the digital-first mindset Estonia is known for.
